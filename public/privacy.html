Privacy, the short version

This site sets no cookies of its own, runs no analytics, and plants no tracking pixels. Nothing watches you read it.

What the form does. When you send a note through the contact form, your name, phone (if you leave one), email, and message go to the restaurant's inbox so someone can answer. Nothing is sold, shared, or added to a mailing list by the form itself.

The map. The visit page shows a Google Maps embed. Loading the map may set Google's own cookies under Google's privacy policy — the site itself sets none.

Reservations. Booking a table happens on OpenTable, under OpenTable's terms and privacy policy; this site just points you there.

Your note. Want a note deleted before it's answered? Say so in a reply to the message — it gets taken out.
