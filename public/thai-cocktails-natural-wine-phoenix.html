The bar

“Things From Home” — drinks with a memory

The cocktail list is drawn from the same place as the food: Chef Gabby's childhood in southern Thailand. Flavors she grew up with, turned into drinks — plus Arizona-made beer and wine and a selection of natural bottles.

DNA

Pandan-infused Mekhong rum, passionfruit liqueur and juice, pineapple, and coconut — the tropical thread running through the whole list.

Banana Espresso Martini

Homme's take on the after-dinner buzz, built with O-liang Thai cold brew.

The wine list

Arizona producers — AZ Stronghold, Flying Leap, and Provision Wines — poured beside natural bottles sourced through The Natural Wine Co.

Beer, too

Arizona-made beer to keep a table of share plates going — diners' guides already tag the bar great for creative cocktails.

“We want to share everything that we create — the food, the cocktails. That's the idea of Homme.” — Chef Vivid “Gabby” Chu, Phoenix New Times

Next door, soon

Blue Zebra — the lounge in the second house

The bar program has a bigger chapter coming: Chef Chu is developing Blue Zebra, a cocktail lounge in the second little red-brick house with a larger drinks menu and southern Thai-influenced bar bites, opening in the coming months with the patio between the houses. Supper first; the lounge is on its way.
