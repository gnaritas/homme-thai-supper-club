Supper is served one way

Order a few dishes. Put them in the middle.

At HOMME, supper is the way it was at home in southern Thailand: good people, a table full of plates, everyone stealing a bite from each other. Something spicy, something comforting, something fresh, something crispy — and always something new to try.

Plates run $30 and under. (GF) gluten-free · (VG) vegetarian. Menu items and descriptions as published by the restaurant; the kitchen adds new dishes as the seasons turn.
